    About this app
    
    
    The AI Allergen Detector screens packaged-food labels and restaurant menus for the
       major food allergens and links each one to its known allergenic biomolecules
       (proteins) using a validated database derived from the
       WHO/IUIS Allergen Nomenclature and AllergenOnline (FARRP).
    
    
      
      
    • Type or paste ingredient/menu text for instant, fully-offline analysis.
      • 
      
    • Scan a photo to read text with on-device OCR (needs internet the first time).
      • 
      
    • Every detection shows the allergen, its protein/biomolecule, scientific class,
          confidence, severity and database source.
      • 
      
    • History can be exported to CSV and PDF for research records.
